FINISHING THE SWEEP. The previous commit fixed 42 of the orphaned claims;
this one takes every remaining unit outside the two reserved sets. 161
claims got their origin line from the claim's OWN `dc 0x...` note - the
Dreamcast roster offset it already records - resolved straight to
file+line in evidence/dreamcast/functions.csv. That is a stronger
derivation than the declarator lookup the last pass used, because the
offset identifies one roster row outright; the declarator form was kept
only for the 17 claims carrying no dc note, and only where the name was
UNIQUE inside the unit's own .obj. 14 were left alone: working names
(`_vslot2`, `GetSize`) that no roster declarator matches, a fourth
TPalette24 constructor when the roster has exactly three, two soundmgr
and one ai_combat body absent from the roster entirely, and remote's
~CNetMsgHandler whose dc note points at an offset with no roster row.

Two arity screens paid off inside the declarator form. bitmap24 has
three constructors and two Draw overloads under one name each; the
roster's param counts (which include `this`) separate them cleanly -
6 params for (name,w,h,source,size), 3 for (name,path), 8 for the
short Draw - so all three resolved uniquely instead of being skipped.

SEERHUT NEEDED A NEW ORIGIN FORM, and it is the single biggest win here.
Its 33 claims are the ten retail quest classes, which the Dreamcast port
does not have: its TSeerHut carries the quest inline and switches on a
type word, and seerhut.obj's roster is 20 rows of a different model. So
no roster row supplies a line, and the file:line form cannot express the
claim without inventing one. parse_unit now also accepts a bare DOS path
with no line:

  // E:\gamedcs\seerhut.cpp

recorded as line 0. This is a strict superset of the old behaviour - the
carve only ever reads the FILE, so a line-less origin anchors exactly as
well - and it cannot collide with prose: the whole comment must be one
DOS path and nothing else, a form that occurs zero times across src/
outside the 33 lines added here.

That one unit was worth more than everything else combined. seerhut was
the sole unspanned candidate in the search..singleselectionpopups gap -
46,782 B holding 182 carved functions - so anchoring it moved 155
functions out of the 3-candidate class and put 106 more under a named
owner.

CORROBORATION. Within a TU, retail address order should reproduce DC
source line order. Over every own-cpp anchor tree-wide with a line
(767 claims, up from the 138 the last pass could check): 7 inversions.
One is pre-existing in `town`, which this commit does not touch. The six
new ones - ai x2, ai_player, hiscore, initialize, resourcemanager - are
each a same-.obj, same-.cpp pair whose DC declarator matches the claim's
declarator by name, so the origin FILE (all the carve reads) is right in
every case and only the two revisions' statement order differs. Two are
independently confirmed as real reorders: ai's get_area_attack_value and
find_attack_hexes sit BEFORE their neighbours in the DC image as well as
in DC line order, and after them in retail.

Checked against DC EMISSION order instead, the same 744 claims give 13
inversions, and the extra ones are all destructor-before-constructor
pairs in units this commit does not touch (border, button, dialogbox,
textwdgt, bottomviewsubwindow). That is the known Dreamcast habit of
appending destructors to the compiland tail - cspriteframe.cpp already
records it - so DC LINE order, not DC address order, is the invariant
worth checking.
